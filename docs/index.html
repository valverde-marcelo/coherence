Why Coherence?

Features that make the difference

🔐

Total Privacy

Your data belongs to you. End-to-end encryption with Ed25519. Nobody can access it without your permission.

🌐

Decentralized

No central server. No company collecting data. You are the full owner of your identity.

⚡

Distributed

Data replicated across multiple peers. No single point of failure. The network keeps working even if a peer goes down.

🚀

Fast

Direct P2P connections. No server latency. Real-time synchronization.

✅

Immutable

Digitally signed posts. Complete history. Impossible to forge or delete.

📱

Modern Interface

Desktop app built with Electron. Intuitive 3-column interface. Easy to use.

Built with Modern Technologies

Robust and scalable architecture

🔗 Hyperswarm

P2P discovery and connection between peers.

📝 Hypercore

Immutable, encrypted logs for your data.

🌳 Hyperbee

Distributed B-tree structure for indexes.

🖥️ Electron

Cross-platform desktop interface.

🔑 Ed25519

Cryptographic digital signing of data.

📦 Node.js

Robust and scalable backend.
